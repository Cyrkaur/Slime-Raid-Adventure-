## Hero gels (`gel/gel_water*`, `gel/gel_fire*`, `gel/gel_plant*`, `gel/gel_hero_base*`)
Built for Slime Raid in Blender 5.2 by `tools/heroes3d/build_hero.py`. That covers the body, face, core, rig, the 6 clips (idle, hop, attack, cast, hit, faint), the water crest, the fire crown and the plant stem. It includes these third-party parts:

| Part | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monstera leaf (plant gel) | Poly Pizza, https://poly.pizza/m/5YAuVDKTti | Isa Lousberg | CC0 1.0 |
| Flower, `Petals_1` from "Flower Bushes" (plant gel) | Poly Pizza, https://poly.pizza/m/1X06RgvSr6 | Quaternius | CC0 1.0 |

The source GLBs are kept in `tools/heroes3d/in/`.
